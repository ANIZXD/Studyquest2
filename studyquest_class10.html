FIRST, UNDERSTAND

When substances become something new

A chemical reaction happens when one or more substances change into new substances. The starting substances are called reactants; the new substances are called products.

Mg+O₂→MgO
💡 Think like a scientist: atoms don't disappear. They rearrange to make new substances. That's why chemical equations must be balanced.

Magnesium reacts with oxygen to form magnesium oxide. When magnesium ribbon burns, it gives off a dazzling white light and leaves a white powder.

Quick memory hook

Reactants → Products. Left side = what you start with. Right side = what you make.
